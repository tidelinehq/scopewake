Privacy

The tool

Tideline collects nothing. It has no telemetry, no accounts and no network code. It runs on your machine and prints to your terminal.

This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers, and has no analytics. The only thing stored in your browser is your light or dark theme choice, in local storage, if you click the theme button. Your host or CDN may keep ordinary server logs; see their policy.
